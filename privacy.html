Legal / Privacy

Privacy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains what information we collect on this website and how we use it. UnboundCompute is in private access, and this site exists mainly to describe the product and let interested teams join the access list.

Information we collect

  • Access list details. When you request access, we collect the email address you submit, along with basic context such as the time and the page you came from.
  • Usage data. Like most websites, we collect standard analytics such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and how you interact with the page.

How we use it

  • To contact you about private access and respond to your request.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We rely on a few third parties to run the site. They process data on our behalf under their own terms:

  • Google Firebase stores access-list submissions.
  • Google Analytics and Google Ads measure traffic and campaign performance.
  • Microsoft Clarity provides aggregate usage analytics.

Cookies and similar technologies

The analytics tools above may set cookies or similar identifiers to measure usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may behave differently if you do.

Data retention

We keep access-list information for as long as needed to evaluate and respond to your interest, and analytics data for as long as the relevant provider retains it. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have given us. To do so, email riyan@unboundcompute.com and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. When we do, we will revise the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: riyan@unboundcompute.com

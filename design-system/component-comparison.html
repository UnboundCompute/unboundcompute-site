Comparison — scanner vs UnboundCompute

A scanner asks

“Is this endpoint open to IDOR?”

It matches patterns against a fixed list. Endless payloads, noisy output, blind to ownership, roles, workflows, and state.

UnboundCompute asks

“Which line does this app trust, and will it hold?”

It models the rules the app is meant to enforce, then designs the exact request that should be stopped at the line. A finding is a boundary that gave way, proven.

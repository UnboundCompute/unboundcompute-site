Help machines think

We are a small, early team building an autonomous security researcher. The work sits where offensive security, agents, and careful engineering meet.

How we work

Small surface area, high standards. We care about proof over noise, we write things down, and we would rather solve the hard reasoning problem than ship another payload list. If a result cannot be demonstrated, we do not claim it.

Where we tend to need people

Security research

Offensive security researcher

You break web apps and APIs for a living and can articulate the reasoning behind a finding, not just the payload. You will help teach the system how to think about trust boundaries.

Engineering

Agent and ML engineer

You build reliable systems on top of language models: planning, tool use, evaluation, and keeping a long-running loop both capable and governed.

Engineering

Product engineer

You turn confirmed findings into something a team trusts: clear evidence, replayable proofs, and tests that wire into a real pipeline.

No open listing that fits?

We hire ahead of formal roles when we meet the right person. If you are strong in any of the areas above, reach out and show us something you have broken or built.

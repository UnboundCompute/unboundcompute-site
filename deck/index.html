The problem

The highest impact bugs require understanding the app.

  • Scanners test a fixed list of known vulnerability types. They have no idea what the application is actually supposed to do.
  • The most damaging issues live in that gap , like broken access control, business logic abuse, and privilege escalation. They depend entirely on context.
  • Finding them means human researchers , scarce, expensive, and impossible to run continuously across everything you ship.
U UnboundCompute02

The idea

Test the assumptions, not the payloads.

A strong researcher models how an application is meant to behave, then designs the one experiment that should be forbidden, and watches what happens.

We are building an autonomous agent that works the same way: understand the app, form the assumptions it makes, try to break each one, and verify the result.

U UnboundCompute03

How it works

A researcher's loop, run on its own.

  • Reasoning engine builds a live model of the app, forms its assumptions, and designs the experiments that would break them.
  • Verification confirms each finding with real, repeatable evidence, a proven contradiction, not a guess.
  • Chaining feeds every confirmed result back in as the next lead, the way an attacker actually works.

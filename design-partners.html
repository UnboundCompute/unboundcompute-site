01 / What partners get

Early, and on your terms.

A

Early access

Private access to UnboundCompute and the modules we have not shipped yet, pointed at a staging environment you choose.

B

Shape the product

Your workflows, your trust boundaries, your reporting. You help decide what we look for, what we prove, and how the evidence reads.

C

Founding pricing

A founding partner rate, locked for your first year when the product opens more widely. The price you start at is the price you keep.

D

A direct line

A shared channel straight to the people building it, and onboarding one to one. You talk to engineers, not a queue.

02 / Who it's for

A good fit looks like this.

Real boundaries

You ship software with real users, real roles, and trust boundaries that matter if they fail.

Security minded

Security, AppSec, or engineering teams who want findings they can act on, not a queue of noise to triage.

Builders

You would rather help shape a tool around how you actually work than wait for the finished version.

A place to point it

A staging or pre production environment you are authorized to test, so we can get to proof quickly and safely.

03 / How it works

From apply to proof.

01Apply

Tell us what you ship and what you would want covered. A short reply is plenty to start.

02Scope

Together we agree on a target you are authorized to test, and what a good result looks like for you.

03Run

We point UnboundCompute at it and work the findings with you, in your channel, against an authorized baseline.

04Shape

What you tell us goes straight into what we build next. That is the whole point of the program.
